03 RevDojo, US
2023 – 2025

BlitzDial, an auto-dialer CRM for car dealerships

I was the technical lead, from architecture through AWS deployment. We replaced a CRM built on ActionHero.js with a Laravel and Vue codebase and moved the production data across. I built the Twilio voice and SMS workflows, the CDK Drive integration for repair orders and service lane data, a mystery-shopping module, and a training LMS. I also explored AI features on LLM APIs.

Laravel, Vue, Twilio, WebSockets, AWS RDS and S3, LLM APIs

2022 – 2024

Translation platform and CRM for a localization company

Internal apps and a platform-style product behind Straker's translation services. I worked on its AI features with Cohere and OpenAI, using Python and SQLAlchemy for the data side, and built Slack and Microsoft Teams apps that connect into the main product. The codebase mixed ColdFusion/ColdBox with Laravel, Vue with TypeScript, and React.

Cohere, OpenAI, Python, SQLAlchemy, Laravel, Vue, React, ColdBox

OCPP, WebSockets, REST APIs, payment gateways
